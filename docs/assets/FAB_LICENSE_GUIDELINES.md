# Fab license guidelines — Prompt 19

## Purpose

Use this guide before importing any third-party Fab asset into Roma Aeterna.

## Rules

1. Do not import an asset until its Fab page shows the price and the selected license tier.
2. Save local evidence: screenshot of asset page, price, license selection, publisher, URL, and acquisition receipt/library entry.
3. Treat every blank, hidden or selector-only license field as `MANUAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`.
4. Do not rely on search snippets for license approval.
5. Do not redistribute raw third-party assets outside the project team.
6. Do not rename vendor assets in place; use wrappers, redirectors created in Unreal, or project-owned aliases.

## Fab Standard License summary

Fab's published standard-license summary permits private/commercial project use, modification for incorporation into projects, and distribution of projects that include assets. It prohibits reselling or redistributing the asset standalone. This is general guidance only: confirm the per-asset license during acquisition.

## Status gates

- `APPROVED`: free, license captured, UE-compatible, useful, quality acceptable, no obvious dependency blocker.
- `CANDIDATE`: useful but missing price/license/technical details.
- `REJECTED`: paid for this milestone, fantasy/inaccurate, unnecessary, too large, or dependency-heavy.
- `MANUAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`: public page lacks enough data or requires login/session to verify.
