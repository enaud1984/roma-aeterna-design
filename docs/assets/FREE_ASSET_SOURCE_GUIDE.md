# Free asset source guide — Prompt 19 modular expansion

Budget asset: **0 euro**. Use sources in this order: CC0, Public Domain, free Fab Standard License, CC BY, then other commercially compatible licenses after review.

| Source | What it offers | Typical license | Risks | Formats | Quality | Download/import | Attribution | Recommended use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Poly Haven | PBR materials, HDRIs, some models | CC0 | Generic surfaces may need historical filtering | texture sets, HDRI, models | High | download official files; import textures/HDRIs and create `MI_RA_*` | not required | roads, stone, plaster, lighting references |
| ambientCG | PBR materials, HDRIs, some models | Public Domain / CC0-style | asset ID must be checked; not all materials are historically exact | JPG/PNG/EXR/SBSAR depending asset | High | download PBR texture set; build material instance | not required | roof tiles, brick, plaster, stone, floors |
| Fab free | Free marketplace assets | Fab Standard License when confirmed | price/license can vary by session; login may be needed | UE packs, GLB, FBX, OBJ | variable | Add to My Library/Add to Project only after license proof | per Fab license evidence | occasional Pompeii scans and UE-ready packs |
| Sketchfab downloadable free | Individual 3D models | CC BY, CC0, or platform Free Standard | license varies per asset; attribution workload | glTF, GLB, FBX, OBJ, BLEND | variable | manual import to sandbox first | often required | columns, arches, amphorae, scans |
| BlenderKit free | Blender assets/materials | mixed, must verify per asset | free/paid filters and license must be checked in BlenderKit account/session | BLEND | variable | export from Blender to FBX/GLB after verification | per asset | filler props/materials only after review |
| Kenney | stylized game assets | CC0 | low-poly style may conflict with realism | OBJ/GLB/FBX depending package | Low/medium | prototype only; replace later | not required | temporary vegetation/rocks/placeholders |
| OpenGameArt | community assets | mixed | many NC/SA/GPL-style assets unsuitable; verify | varied | variable | import only after license audit | often required | fallback placeholders only |
| Wikimedia Commons | reference photos and some models | per-file; CC BY-SA common | share-alike/attribution may be complex; per-file review mandatory | images, media, some models | variable | reference/decal only after license approval | usually required | opus references, inscriptions, historical texture reference |
| Smithsonian Open Access | museum scans/images | CC0 where marked Open Access | object-specific rights must be checked | 3D scans/images | High | manual import/reference after object page check | provenance retained | props/statues/reference |
| The Met Open Access | public-domain images/data and some 3D models | CC0 for Open Access public-domain works | object-specific Open Access icon/status must be checked | images/data/3D where available | High | reference/decal/model import after object check | not required but provenance retained | motifs, statues, fresco/mosaic reference |
| NASA | imagery/media references | generally public domain with logo/people restrictions | not Roman-specific; logo/endorsement restrictions | images/video | High | sky/reference only after guideline check | provenance retained | sky/reference, not architecture |
| Other CC0 libraries | materials/models | CC0 | provenance and mirror trust | varied | variable | official download only | not required | fill missing generic modules |

Do not use CC BY-NC, editorial-only, extracted videogame assets, unclear licenses, subscription-only assets, trials, or assets with no provenance.
